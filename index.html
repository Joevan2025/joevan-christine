    Good to know

    Questions you might have

    When should we reply by?

    By 15 October 2026, please. That gives us time to settle seating and the dinner count with the venue.

    What time should we arrive at the church?

    The Mass begins at 2:00 PM. Arriving by 1:30 PM means you are comfortably seated before the processional.

    Where do we park?

    Parking is free at the Redemptorist lot beside the church and on the grounds of Nature's Village Resort.

    What is the theme?

    Romantic garden elegance, in pastel purple, pastel blue and sage green. Formal or semi-formal attire in those colours is perfect. See the colours.

    Can we take photos during the ceremony?

    We are having an unplugged ceremony. Please keep cameras and phones away during the Mass so everyone can be fully present; our photographer will capture it. At the reception, snap as many as you like and tag #DisTINedtobewithJOE.

    Can I bring a plus-one?

    Our seats are counted by name, so plus-ones are by invitation only. If your invitation includes one, it will say so. Do message us if you are unsure.
